Cookies & browser storage

Effective 1 November 2026

The current portfolio does not set first-party cookies for analytics or advertising. It uses one local browser-storage preference to remember the appearance you selected.

What this site stores

  • portfolio-theme: a local-storage value of “light” or “dark,” used to keep your visual theme between visits. It stays in your browser and is not sent to the portfolio.
  • Temporary interface state: an optional project preview loads only after you press its “Load preview” button. This choice is held in page memory and is not saved as a cookie or local-storage preference. Press “Unload preview” to remove it.

Optional third-party content

Loading an embedded Recipe Central or AssureX preview makes a request to that separate site. Its owner or host may set cookies or use other browser technologies under their own policy. The preview is not contacted until you select it. Choosing a non-English reading language sends visible portfolio text to Google Cloud Translation through the portfolio server; this service may process request data under Google’s own terms. Translation requests are made only after you select a language. You can keep previews unloaded, or open a project directly in a new tab instead.

Analytics and consent

No analytics or advertising tracking tools are included in the current portfolio code, so there are no optional tracking cookies to accept here. The explicit “Load preview” and language selection actions are the choice points for external content and translation processing. If optional tracking is added later, this notice and the consent controls will be updated before it is enabled.

Managing storage

You can clear the portfolio theme, language, text-size, saved-project, recently explored project, and recently visited section preferences through your browser’s site-data settings. The admin editor may use tab session storage to preserve an unpublished draft until that tab closes. Clearing or blocking storage resets these preferences but does not prevent browsing the portfolio. Offline caching stores site files in your browser cache; you can remove it through the same browser site-data controls.

The privacy practices of an external website are outside this portfolio’s control. See the privacy notice for details about previews and contact links.
