Privacy notice

Effective 1 November 2026

This notice describes the current Tegabyte portfolio website: what it handles, why, and what happens when you choose to contact me or load an external project preview. It is written for this site’s current features, not for the separate Recipe Central or AssureX websites.

Who is responsible

Oghenetega Oyibocha Emmanuel, using the professional name Tegabyte, is the contact for privacy questions about this portfolio. Email oyibochaoghenetega5@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 704 717 5423. This portfolio does not publish a street address or claim a separate registered company.

What the portfolio handles

  • Theme preference: the site stores your light or dark preference in browser local storage under portfolio-theme. It is not sent to me by the portfolio code.
  • AI Lab tools: text typed into the retrieval demo and changes made in the diagrams are processed in your browser. The current tools do not send that content to a model API or portfolio server.
  • Messages you choose to send: when you follow an email or WhatsApp link, the information you include is handled by the relevant email or messaging provider and delivered to me. The site has no contact submission form, account, newsletter, or checkout.
  • Technical requests: the hosting or network provider may process technical request data, such as an IP address, browser details, and request time, to deliver and protect the site. Its logs and retention are governed by that provider’s terms; I do not control their exact retention period.

External previews and links

Recipe Central and AssureX previews are not loaded until you press Load preview. That choice makes a direct connection to the selected external website. The project site and its hosting provider may receive connection information and apply their own cookies or privacy practices. You can press Unload preview to remove the embedded content, use the clearly labelled link to open the project in a separate tab, or leave the preview unloaded. Social, certificate, and project links also take you to third-party services whose handling is outside this portfolio.

Tracking, cookies, and sharing

The current portfolio source includes no analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural tracking tags, or first-party tracking cookies. Optional browser storage keeps your theme, text-size choice, saved project list, and recently explored projects or page sections on your device. The CMS uses tab session storage for an admin project draft while you edit; it is not sent to the portfolio server until you publish. Offline access may cache site files in your browser. These features are not used to identify you or track activity across sites. You can clear them through your browser’s site-data settings. I do not sell personal data. Information is shared with the providers needed to deliver the site or the communication you start, and where disclosure is required by law.

Retention and security

The portfolio itself has no visitor database or form inbox. Messages you send may remain in the email or messaging service and in my account while they are useful for responding or keeping a business record; I remove them when no longer needed, subject to any legal requirement. No online service can be promised completely risk-free, so please do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or sensitive information through a general enquiry.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid optional project embeds, unload a preview after opening it, clear the theme preference in your browser storage, and choose what to include in a message. Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies, you may have rights concerning access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, and complaints to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact me by email to make a privacy request. The official Act and NDPC website provide more information. Rights and lawful grounds depend on the circumstances.

Children and changes

This portfolio is a professional website and is not designed to collect information from children. I may revise this notice when site features or applicable requirements change; the effective date above will be updated.

This is a plain-language notice of the site’s current implementation, not a representation that the portfolio is certified or that every third-party provider follows the same practices.
